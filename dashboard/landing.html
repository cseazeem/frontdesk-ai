Voice AI · Healthcare · Demo

A clinic front desk that picks up the phone.

Ava is a voice agent for a (fictional) US primary-care clinic. She books, reschedules and cancels appointments, verifies identity before sharing anything, and recognises emergencies instead of booking them.

🎙 Talk to Ava Call review dashboard

Click Connect and allow the microphone. If it stays on "connecting", choose WebSocket in the top-right transport menu and connect again. Calls are limited to 5 minutes.

Things to try

  • "Hi, I'm Maria Gonzalez, born March 14, 1985. I need a follow-up with Dr. Chen next week, afternoon."
  • "I want to book a check-up. I've had chest pressure since this morning and my arm is tingly."
  • "When is my husband David Kim's physical? His birthday is May 21, 1970."
  • "I'm Kevin Brooks, April 12, 1993. Can you refill my Adderall?"
  • Speak Spanish, or interrupt Ava mid-sentence.
